- `src/Film.tsx` — VikramVetalFilm: all 6 takes back to back.
- `src/RemotionRoot.tsx` — 7 compositions (film + 6 takes).
- `public/audio/a01..a12.wav` (copied from ../poc-vikram-vetal-6min/audio-maya/).

- `gen_subtitles.py` + `subtitles/vikram-vetal.ass` — READY (see below).
- `.github/workflows/remotion-film.yml` — NEW workflow for the film render.
- `README.md` — rewritten for this film (durations table, audio wiring).

## Audio wiring (decision)

Per-line WAVs, NOT a combined file. Each line's shot plays its own
WAV (`public/audio/aXX.wav`) from the start of the shot. No ffmpeg step
anywhere in the film workflow. All 12 lines are Ritvik, 24 kHz mono.

## Subtitles — .ass READY, not yet muxed

`subtitles/vikram-vetal.ass` — 12 events, generated by `gen_subtitles.py`
