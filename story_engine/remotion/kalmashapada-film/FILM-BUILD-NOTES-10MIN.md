# Kalmashapada 10-minute film — build notes (for the commit + dispatch step)

Prepared 2026-11-24. NOT committed, NOT dispatched — preparation only.
Topic #3 in the mythology-horror series (Ashwatthama #1, Vikram-Vetal #2).
The 10-minute version of the short film in `poc-kalmashapada-2d/`
(1 min 22 s, rendered + uploaded 23 Nov 2026). The short version

## What was built

`poc-kalmashapada-10min-2d/` is the full 10-minute film, adapted from the
short film — same engine, same frame math, same gap rule, no photos
(100% code-drawn):
- `src/data/takes.ts` — single source of truth: 5 takes × 11 lines,
  55 lines b01–b55, all durations from `../poc-kalmashapada-10min/
  durations.json` (total narration 637.36 s). The script file's header
  wrongly says "40 lines"; the real count is 55.
- `src/components/CodeScenes.tsx` — 38 scenes: 19 reused from the short
  film + 19 new (see below). New helpers: Queen (maroon robe + veil),
  Cow (Kamadhenu Nandini), SeatedSage (meditation). King gained an
  optional `sword` prop (default true); DemonKing's horns were redrawn
  a third time (see stills notes).
- `src/components/LineScene.tsx` — one line = one shot + its own WAV;
  switch covers all 37 scenes used (the `transform` close-up from the
  short film is kept in CodeScenes but unused in this film).
- `src/components/Cards.tsx`, `src/takes/T01..T05.tsx`,
  `src/components/StandardTake.tsx`, `src/Film.tsx`,
  `src/RemotionRoot.tsx` — unchanged in structure; only frame counts
  and comments updated.
- `public/audio/b01..b55.wav` (copied from
  `../poc-kalmashapada-10min/audio-maya-a/` + `audio-maya-b/` — WAVs
  only, NO mp3s). All 55 verified 24 kHz mono (ffprobe, every file).
- `public/photos/title.jpg + end.jpg` (regenerated by `make_cards.py`,
  same cards as the short film; Zero Spoiler Rule — neither card shows
  the twist).
- `gen_subtitles.py` + `subtitles/kalmashapada-10min.ass` — 55 events
  (see below).
- `.github/workflows/remotion-film.yml` — audio check updated to
  b*.wav / b01.wav / b55.wav; header updated to 20233 frames, ~11.2 min.
  PROJECT_DIR left exactly as in the short film's file (parent handles
  the repo-root copy).
- `README.md` — rewritten for the 10-min film.

## Audio wiring (decision)

Per-line WAVs, NOT a combined file. Each line's shot plays its own
WAV (`public/audio/bXX.wav`) from the start of the shot. No ffmpeg step
anywhere in the film workflow. All 55 lines are Ritvik, 24 kHz mono.
Audio is 30 MB total (55 WAVs).
