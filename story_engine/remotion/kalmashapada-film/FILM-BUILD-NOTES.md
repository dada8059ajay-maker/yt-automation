## What was built

`poc-kalmashapada-2d/` is the full film, adapted from `poc-vikram-vetal-2d/`:
- `src/data/takes.ts` — single source of truth: 5 takes, 19 lines, all
  durations from ffprobe-measured WAVs (total narration 62.88 s).
- `src/components/Atmosphere.tsx` — Embers / Fog / GlowPulse / Stars /
  Vignette (copied unchanged from Vikram-Vetal; fog opacity 0.022).
- `src/components/CodeScenes.tsx` — 19 code-drawn scenes, one per line
  (throne, friends, hunt, narrow path, standoff, sage speaks, curse,
  rakshasa, feast, transformation, forest demon, spotted feet, eats
  Shakti, hundred sons, liberator, freedom, twist, mirror, hands of light).
  100% code-drawn — NO photos in this film. New helpers: Sage (pale robe,
  beard, staff), DemonKing (horned, red eyes).
- `src/components/LineScene.tsx` — one line = one shot + its own WAV.
- `src/components/Cards.tsx` — title card (3.5 s, T01) + end card (5 s, T05).
- `src/takes/T01..T05.tsx` + `StandardTake.tsx` (all 5 takes use it).
- `src/Film.tsx` — KalmashapadaFilm: all 5 takes back to back.
- `src/RemotionRoot.tsx` — 6 compositions (film + 5 takes).
- `public/audio/a01..a19.wav` (copied from ../poc-kalmashapada-6min/audio-maya/).
- `public/photos/title.jpg + end.jpg` (made by `make_cards.py`, PIL +
  Noto Sans Devanagari; Zero Spoiler Rule — neither card shows the twist).
- `make_cards.py` — title/end card generator (adapted from Vikram-Vetal).
- `gen_subtitles.py` + `subtitles/kalmashapada.ass` — READY (see below).
- `.github/workflows/remotion-film.yml` — NEW workflow for the film render.
- `README.md` — rewritten for this film (durations table, audio wiring).

## Audio wiring (decision)

Per-line WAVs, NOT a combined file. Each line's shot plays its own
WAV (`public/audio/aXX.wav`) from the start of the shot. No ffmpeg step
anywhere in the film workflow. All 19 lines are Ritvik, 24 kHz mono.

## Subtitles — .ass READY, not yet muxed

`subtitles/kalmashapada.ass` — 19 events, generated by `gen_subtitles.py`
from the same timeline numbers as `takes.ts` (title lead 3.5 s, then
each line starts at its shot start, ends at start + audioSec; last
subtitle ends 0:01:16.13, film ends 0:01:21.70). Mux it into the
rendered mp4 with ffmpeg + a Devanagari font AFTER the GitHub render.
ffmpeg drawtext is never used (Devanagari conjuncts break).

## Stills verification

21 stills rendered and EYE-CHECKED at 960x540, v1 in `out/stills/`
(frames 50, 151, 268, 380, 503, 629, 741, 873, 1002, 1115, 1192, 1287,
1454, 1590, 1670, 1774, 1885, 1990, 2106, 2234, 2400 — one per shot +
both cards).

v1 findings (all fixed in v2): title card's long second line overflowed
past both frame edges (split into 3 lines at font 130); throne back read
as a doorway arch (narrowed, seat + armrests added); ministers read as
small pawn-cones (scaled up, warm brown robes, gold chest bands); the
narrow path read as a pale hill (edge lines + dashed centre line added);
the rakshasa wisp read as a UFO with a grin (redrawn as a comet with a
smoke tail streaming behind); the transformation close-up was cropped
too tight with a flat crown bar (scale 3.4 -> 2.3, crown redrawn as an
arc band); the demon's horns read as rabbit ears (short, wide,
outward-curving bull horns); the demon was too small/dark in the forest
(scale 1.5 -> 1.75 + red aura); the floor rect was painted OVER the
spotted feet (draw order fixed); the fallen sage read as a surfboard,
then as a flat rug (redrawn bigger, with head + beard + arm + diagonal
staff, then moved clear of the demon); the cupped hands read as
dumplings with a floating orb (redrawn as two forearms with palms up
and four finger capsules each, orb nestled between them).

v2 findings (fixed in v3): the throne seat still read as a red bar
across the king's waist (king moved to stand BESIDE the throne);
the crown arc was right but the face still filled the frame (kept —
the arc crown now reads); the feet merged into one bar (redrawn as two
separate front-view stubs with a gap); the fallen sage was hidden
behind the demon (moved right, clear of the demon); the hands were
still blobs (forearm + finger-capsule rebuild).

v3 re-check: all 5 re-rendered frames verified good. Title card clean
Devanagari, no spoiler. Fog reads as faint mist. The demon's horns now
read as horns in every scene (a11, a13, a14, a15, a16, a18).

Known limits (not fixed — noted, not hidden): the fallen sage's body
is still a fairly flat shape (head + beard + staff now sell it as a
person); the demon's cloak is a plain dark cone (reads as a demon
silhouette, not a rabbit, now that the horns are right); dark figures
blend into the dark ground at the waist in stills (the walk itself is
visible in motion); no lip-sync (silhouette only); no on-screen text
inside scenes.
